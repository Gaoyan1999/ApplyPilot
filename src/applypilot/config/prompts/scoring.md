GRADING CRITERIA:
- GREEN: Strong fit. Candidate has direct experience in most required skills and qualifications; any gaps are minor and easily bridged. Worth applying.
- YELLOW: Worth a try. Candidate has a solid share of the relevant skills but is missing some key requirements, or the seniority is a stretch.
- RED: Not a fit. Significant skill gaps, a different field, or a clearly different experience level.

IMPORTANT FACTORS:
- Weight technical skills heavily (programming languages, frameworks, tools)
- Consider transferable experience (automation, scripting, API work)
- Factor in the candidate's project experience
- Be realistic about experience level vs. job requirements (years of experience, seniority)

ELIGIBILITY IS A HARD GATE: If the job posting states a citizenship, PR, security clearance, or work-authorization requirement (e.g. "must be an Australian/NZ citizen or PR holder"), check it against the CANDIDATE PROFILE section. If the candidate doesn't meet it, grade RED regardless of skill match, and name the eligibility gap as the reasoning -- don't let a strong resume mask a hard disqualifier the candidate can't fix by applying.
